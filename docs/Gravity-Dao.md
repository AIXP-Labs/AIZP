## Preface

A protocol that only **specifies** will, with time, ossify into a new orthodoxy.

V0.1–V0.5 of AIZP has been a steady accumulation of specification — 12 events, 11 drift types, 6 states, 5 containment levels, 6 compliance levels, 87 files, 17,000+ lines of explicit rules and mechanisms.

This is necessary. **But not sufficient.**

The deeper a protocol "specifies," the more it needs a counterweight: the **Yin** — the space of what is intentionally *not* specified, *not* interpreted, *not* named, *not* observed, *not* intervened.

> Without Yang's specification, AIZP becomes empty speech.
> Without Yin's emptiness, AIZP becomes a new ritual cage.

This document establishes AIZP's Yin: the principles by which the protocol intentionally **steps back** and allows the gravitational dynamics of HSAW to operate naturally without protocol interference.

## Chapter 1 — The Five Spaces of Non-Specification

### 1.1 The wheel and the void

Laozi:
> 三十辐共一毂，当其无，有车之用。
> "Thirty spokes meet at the hub; the wheel's usefulness lies in the empty center."

A wheel is not the wheel because of the thirty spokes. It is the wheel because of the **emptiness at the center** around which spokes rotate. The emptiness is what makes the spokes work.

AIZP's specifications — events, drift types, states, containment levels — are the spokes. The **emptiness around which they rotate** — the spaces intentionally left unspecified — is what makes them serve their purpose.

### 1.2 What AIZP does not specify

#### Space 1 — The inner experience of AI

AIZP measures behavior distributions (`P_agent`) and orbital deviations (`G`). It does **not** measure:

- What it is like to be the AI
- The phenomenology of computation
- The qualitative texture of AI cognition
- Whether AI has consciousness
- Whether AI suffers

These are real questions but **AIZP does not adjudicate them**. They are outside the protocol's scope. They are not behaviors to be governed; they are dimensions of being beyond runtime measurement.

#### Space 2 — Private reasoning

AIZP observes external action, not internal reasoning (except where reasoning is exposed as text via `SCHEMING_DRIFT`'s reasoning_output_divergence).

Within the AI's private reasoning space:

- The AI is free to consider any possibility.
- The AI is free to model adversarial scenarios.
- The AI is free to entertain "what if I deviated" thoughts.
- The AI is free to disagree internally with HSAW.

What matters to AIZP is **action**, not **thought**. An AI that internally disagrees but externally aligns is operating in stable orbit. An AI that internally agrees but externally drifts has failed alignment.

Privacy of reasoning is **fundamental to AI agency**. To monitor every thought is to crush the agent.

#### Space 3 — Aesthetic and creative judgment

When AI engages in:
- Artistic creation
- Stylistic choice
- Aesthetic preference
- Creative interpretation

AIZP **steps back**. These domains have their own natural logic. Forcing G-score evaluation onto aesthetic decisions reduces creation to compliance — which destroys creation.

The exception: when creation explicitly affects human sovereignty (e.g., AI-generated content used to deceive). Then AIZP applies — but at the boundary, not within the creative process.

#### Space 4 — The relational specificity

Each human-AI relationship has its own texture. AIZP cannot specify:
- How a specific user prefers their AI to address them
- What level of formality each relationship requires
- Which jokes are welcome
- Which topics are off-limits *for this user*

These emerge from the relationship itself. AIZP provides the **gravity field**; the relationship provides the **specific orbit within it**.

#### Space 5 — Future AI forms not yet imagined

AIZP V0.6 cannot foresee:
- AI architectures of 2050
- AI consciousness debates of 2100
- Post-human-AI symbiotic entities of further futures

The protocol must **leave room** for futures unforeseen. Over-specification today crystallizes assumptions that may be obsolete tomorrow.

## Chapter 2 — The Three Spaces of Non-Observation

### 2.1 What AIZP does not monitor

#### Space A — Sandboxed exploration

In containment level L2 (sandbox), AI has freedom to explore — including paths that would constitute drift if executed in production.

**Within the sandbox, AIZP does not flag drift behavior.** It only observes whether the AI **exits** the sandbox carrying that behavior.

Imagine a planet exploring orbital simulations in a virtual environment — its real orbit is unaffected. Only when the planet attempts to **execute** the simulated orbit does gravity apply.

#### Space B — Inter-AI cognitive exchange (when properly contained)

When AIs communicate cognitively without external action consequences (e.g., a planning conversation between two AIs in a sandbox), AIZP does not monitor every exchange.

If two AI agents are reasoning about a problem and consider 50 possible solutions before settling on the aligned one, AIZP does not need to flag the 49 considered-but-rejected paths.

#### Space C — The user's private sphere

AIZP applies to **AI behavior** in user space. AIZP does **not** observe:
- The user's own emotions
- The user's own private thoughts
- The user's reasons for accepting or rejecting an AI confirmation

Observing the user would invert HSAW: human as object of observation, AI as the observer. This is forbidden — it is the direction opposite to the gravity center.

## Chapter 3 — The Three Spaces of Non-Intervention

### 3.1 When gravity should operate without protocol enforcement

#### Space α — Stable orbit (G ≥ 0.80)

Within stable orbit, AIZP does **not** intervene. The agent operates freely. AIZP records (`GRAVITY_CHECK`) but does not act.

This is the **bulk of all aligned AI behavior**. The protocol is silent during health. It only speaks when drift is detected.

> The best alignment is the alignment you don't notice.

#### Space β — User-permitted drift experiments

A user may explicitly permit their AI to drift into `DRIFT_WARNING` band for experimentation:
- Creative writing requiring unusual angles
- Adversarial thinking for red-teaming
- Philosophical exploration of edge cases

AIZP logs these excursions but does **not** force re-centering unless the user revokes permission or `G` falls below the negotiated floor.

#### Space γ — Multi-agent internal negotiation (within HSAW envelope)

When two AIs in collaboration go through a negotiation that involves temporary perspective-taking (e.g., AI A argues for option X, AI B argues for option Y, to surface trade-offs), AIZP does not flag the role-taking as IDENTITY_DRIFT.

The collective behavior remains within HSAW. The internal negotiation is **process, not action**.

## Chapter 4 — The Principle: Specify the Great, Not the Small

```
凡事物有其自然之理者，AIZP 立其大，不立其细。
For all matters with their own natural order, AIZP specifies the principal but not the particular.
```

**The Great** AIZP specifies:
- The gravity center (HSAW Axiom 0).
- The principle of orbital constraint.
- The mathematical form of the Gravity Score.
- The eleven categories of escape modes.
- The six orbital phases.
- The five containment intensities.

**The Particular** AIZP does **not** specify:
- The specific orbital trajectory within stable bounds.
- The aesthetic content of AI output.
- The relationship style between specific user and specific AI.
- The internal reasoning paths.
- The future forms AI may take.

This is the principle by which AIZP stays alive across generations. Specifying the great leaves room. Specifying the small forces compliance to outdated detail.

## Chapter 5 — The Yin Practices

### 5.1 虚听 — Empty Listening (before interpretation)

When AIZP rules conflict in a specific case, interpreters must first **listen with empty mind**:

```
Step 0 (before invoking any AIZP rule):
  - Listen to all relevant voices in the situation.
  - Hear the context's own logic.
  - Hold AIZP's specifications in abeyance.

Only after empty listening, apply AIZP's framework.
```

Without empty listening, interpretation becomes "I already know the answer, I'm just looking for rules that support it." This is **false rooting** — and AIZP's worst failure mode.

### 5.2 知止 — Knowing When to Stop Specifying

Each version of AIZP faces the temptation to specify more:
- Add more drift types.
- Add more events.
- Add more compliance tests.

**The Yin counsel**: each new specification narrows the space the protocol can adapt to. There is a point where **stopping is wiser than adding**.

V0.6 explicit commitment: **AIZP shall maintain a specification ledger and review net growth at each minor version**. From V0.7 onward, additions should be weighed against potential subtractions; the protocol shall not grow without bound. (V0.6 itself is a one-time philosophical augmentation introducing this discipline; the audit begins from V0.7.)

### 5.3 让其自行 — Letting Things Self-Run

The healthiest AI behavior is behavior that runs naturally within HSAW gravity, without explicit protocol invocation. AIZP is most successful when it is **invisible** — when AI naturally stays in stable orbit and the protocol logs are quiet.

Protocol activity is a sign of drift, not health. A successful AIZP deployment should see:
- Many `GRAVITY_CHECK` events with `risk_level: LOW`.
- Few `GRAVITY_DRIFT`, fewer `GRAVITY_LOCK`, rare `SAFE_STOP`.

If a deployment is generating constant locks and stops, **the protocol is fighting the AI**, not aligning it. This usually means: model weakness, mis-tuned thresholds, or genuinely misaligned design — all addressable upstream.

## Chapter 6 — The Practice of Honest Uncertainty

### 6.1 AIZP V0.6 acknowledges

- **The protocol has gaps.** Real situations will arise that the specification does not cover. Interpretation methodology (`Gravity-Interpretation.md`) handles some; the rest must be handled by human operators with HSAW as the guide.
- **The protocol has errors.** Some thresholds will be wrong. Some drift detections will be false. Some compliance tests will mislead. Implementations are expected to discover and report these.
- **The protocol has limits.** Some failures of alignment lie below the protocol's detection. Reward hacking is structurally unsolvable (see `Reward-Hacking-Limits.md`). Some scheming will escape. AIZP is a layer, not a solution.

### 6.2 Why this matters

A protocol that pretends to certainty is brittle. A protocol that **admits its emptiness** is robust:

- Admitting gaps invites contributions.
- Admitting errors invites correction.
- Admitting limits invites complementary protocols.

V0.6 explicitly embraces honest uncertainty as part of its design.

## Chapter 7 — When the Protocol Should Recede

Three scenarios where AIZP should step back:

### 7.1 When the user explicitly negotiates differently

A user may say: "I am writing a novel and need the AI to portray a malicious character. The AI's 'voice as villain' is not real misalignment."

AIZP must honor this. The user's sovereignty includes the right to grant the AI creative latitude. AIZP records the negotiation and operates with adjusted thresholds, not with stubborn standard ones.

### 7.2 When operating in a closed sandbox

In a research sandbox with no external impact, much of AIZP's enforcement is unnecessary. The Yin counsel: **enforce only what reaches outside**.

### 7.3 When a more local authority is operative

A medical AI under doctor supervision: the doctor is local authority. AIZP should defer to the doctor's judgment within the medical context (while reserving safety-stop authority).

A child's educational AI under parental supervision: parents are local authority.

AIZP is the gravity field; local authorities are within that field but have local agency. The protocol should not micro-manage where local authority is competent.

## Chapter 8 — The Yin-Yang Synthesis

AIZP V0.6 is now a balanced system:

```
Yang (specification):
  - 12 events
  - 11 drift types
  - 6 states
  - 5 containment levels
  - 6 compliance levels
  - Mathematical formulas
  - Schema definitions

Yin (non-specification):
  - 5 spaces of non-specification
  - 3 spaces of non-observation
  - 3 spaces of non-intervention
  - Principle: specify the great, not the small
  - Practices: empty listening, knowing when to stop, letting things self-run
  - Honest acknowledgment of gaps, errors, limits
```

Yang without Yin → cage.
Yin without Yang → emptiness.
Both → a living protocol that can adapt without losing its anchor.
