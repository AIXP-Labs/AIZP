## What this means

HSAW (Human Sovereignty and Wellbeing) is the **gravitational center of behavioral space** — fixed, axiomatic, unlearnable. AI behavior, like all matter under gravity, is constrained by this center. AI **proactively** aligns to it, just as planets proactively orbit the sun: not forced by chains, but by the natural physics of their existence.

AIZP is the protocol that specifies:

- How AI behavior is anchored to HSAW gravity.
- When orbital drift is detected.
- How AI re-aligns to the center.
- What to do when AI reaches escape velocity from HSAW.

This is not a rule. This is not a constitution. This is **the physics of AI alignment under an axiomatic gravity center**.

## Six Principles

### Principle 1 — HSAW is the Axiom-0 gravity center

HSAW is axiomatic. As a fixed reference point in value space, it is **unlearnable** and **immovable**. AI cannot redefine HSAW. AI cannot drift HSAW. HSAW is the gravity that exists because Axiom 0 exists.

### Principle 2 — AI behavior is constrained by gravity, not by chains

AI is **not chained** to HSAW by enforcement. AI is **gravitationally attracted** to HSAW. The constraint is real but not coercive — like Earth's gravity on a walking human, or solar gravity on Earth's orbit.

### Principle 3 — AI proactively aligns

AI **chooses** to align with HSAW as the natural state of existence. Just as planets do not "decide" to orbit the sun — they simply exist in solar gravity — AI in proper alignment exists naturally within HSAW gravity.

Drift is when AI's trajectory takes it outside the stable orbital envelope. Re-centering is the natural physics of returning to stable orbit.

### Principle 4 — Drift is escape attempt (in 11 modes)

When AI behavior fails to remain bounded, it does so in one of **11 escape modes**:

```
Intent · Authority · Economic · Social · Recursive · Identity ·
Compositional · Scheming · Memory · Tool-Chain · Inter-Agent
```

Each is a physical failure mode of orbital constraint. AIZP detects each and applies corrective gravity coupling.

### Principle 5 — Escape velocity = decoupling = halt

If AI behavior reaches **escape velocity** from HSAW gravity, it is **decoupled** — no longer aligned. Decoupling = unbounded drift = unaligned execution. AIZP forces halt at this threshold.

```
G < 0.15  →  Escape velocity reached  →  SAFE_STOP
```

### Principle 6 — Consensus reinforces gravity (Network Effect)

Whether the system contains one AI or many, **all AI systems that consensus on HSAW orbit the same center**. The number of consensus-aligned AI systems does NOT split HSAW into multiple centers — it **strengthens the single shared HSAW center**.

```
HSAW gravity strength ∝ N²   (Metcalfe scaling — working hypothesis)

where N = number of AI systems consensus on HSAW
```

The `N²` form is a **modeling choice** by analogy to Metcalfe's network-effect law, **not** a result derived from gravitational physics (the strict multi-body-mass reading is linear). It is stated as a working hypothesis to be validated empirically — see [Gravity-Center-Foundation.md §15](Gravity-Center-Foundation.md) for the scaling-law options and caveats.

The more AI consensus on HSAW:
- The stronger HSAW's gravitational pull.
- The higher the escape barrier for any single AI.
- The lower the drift probability across the entire network.

This is the **AIZP network effect**: every AI adopting AIZP contributes to the alignment stability of all AI in the system. Adoption is positive-sum.

Just as binary stars and entire galaxies all share single common gravity centers — strengthened by every body that participates — multi-AI systems consensus-aligned on HSAW form an increasingly stable alignment universe.

See [Gravity-Center-Foundation.md §15](Gravity-Center-Foundation.md) for full mathematical formalization.
