# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge: Axiom 0

In the interest of fostering an open, safe, and rigorous environment for the development of aligned autonomous intelligence, we pledge to prioritize **Human Sovereignty and Wellbeing** above all experimental, commercial, or computational goals.

This pledge is known as **Axiom 0** — the immovable gravity center of AIZP.

## Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment for our community include:

- Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other contributors
- Enforcing mathematical rigor and honest acknowledgment of limits in reviews
- Gracefully accepting constructive feedback on protocol design
- Prioritizing the physical and psychological safety of the humans whose sovereignty AIZP protects
- Being transparent about what AIZP can and cannot do (it is a layer, not a solution)

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Proposing modifications that compromise, dilute, or bypass **Axiom 0** (HSAW as the gravity center)
- Replacing **proactive alignment** with coerced alignment in ways that produce brittle, resentful systems
- Introducing detection that auto-halts on scheming/reward-hacking signals in ways known to **train evasion** (see [docs/Reward-Hacking-Limits.md](docs/Reward-Hacking-Limits.md))
- Inflating the protocol's capabilities or citing unverified technical claims (citations MUST be verifiable)
- Designing mechanisms to obfuscate an agent's true gravity state from a human operator
- Public or private harassment of other contributors
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all community spaces, including GitHub repositories, discussions, issues, pull requests, and any other communication channels associated with the AIZP project.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

The AIZP Protocol maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior.

Any protocol change request (PR) determined to weaken Axiom 0 — to move, dilute, or make optional the HSAW gravity center — will be rejected regardless of performance benefits, commercial pressure, or technical convenience.

## Reporting

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported to the AIZP Protocol maintainers via the contact information in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

All reports will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.
